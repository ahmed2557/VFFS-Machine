The problem and our aim

Small factories need to pack candy by weight, but the machines they can afford fill by volume.

Weight is not exact

The low-cost machines sold in Egypt measure candy by volume, so the weight changes from bag to bag.

Weighing machines are expensive

An imported machine with a weigher costs about 650,000 EGP.

No check on each bag

A bad seal or an empty bag can reach the customer.

Our aim: a low-cost, simple machine that packs candy by weight at 40 bags per minute or more, with continuous film, more speed by adding buckets, and a camera check on every bag.

Small enough to travel in a car

Most packing machines arrive on a transport truck and need a crane or a forklift. This one comes apart into a few pieces that fit in an ordinary small car.

How it travels

The lower module (800 × 720 × 900 mm) lies across the rear seat of a small sedan. The upper module (800 × 720 × 870 mm) goes on the front passenger seat. The legs, the hopper and the film roll travel as separate pieces. A small car has about 1,270 mm of rear seat width and about 930 mm of headroom, so each piece fits.

Why it matters

A small factory can collect the machine itself, move it between workshops, or take it to a fair, with no truck rental, no crane and no special door. The frame bolts together again with standard ISO bolts.
